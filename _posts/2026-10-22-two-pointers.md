The two pointers pattern is a common technique used in solving array and string problems. It involves using two pointers to traverse the data structure, often starting from different positions and moving towards each other or in the same direction. This pattern is particularly useful for problems that require _comparing elements or finding specific values_ within a **sorted array** or **string**.
