Hi, this is my first post about Leetcode Patterns. During the following week, you will find a series of post with the category "Leetcode Patterns" and the tag "Python". The purpose of this series is to document the most common patterns that DSA problems follow, and how to solve them using Python. 
    
You will also sometimes find at the end of each problem a mistake list of things that I could have done better, and that I will try to avoid in the future. I hope you enjoy it, and if you have any questions or suggestions, please contact me through my email: [risardo01@gmail.com](mailto:risardo01@gmail.com)

I recommend if you will follow this series of posts to study, you should already have basic knowledge of Python and DSA. Each post will have 2-3 problems per pattern, that means that if you cant solve the first one, look the solution, study it, write your own solution without copying it, and try the following problem for the pattern.
