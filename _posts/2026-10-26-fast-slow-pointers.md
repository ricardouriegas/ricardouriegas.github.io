The fast and slow pointers pattern is a common technique used in solving linked list and cycle detection problems. It involves using two pointers that traverse the data structure at different speeds, typically with one pointer moving faster than the other. This pattern is particularly useful for problems that require _detecting cycles or finding specific nodes_ within a **linked list** or **graph**.
